# docs/

Results: the write-ups, the figures, and the evidence recorded on the robot. Results that
a script produced are regenerated by re-running it. Robot recordings cannot be
regenerated, so the files here are the only copy.

**Reading order for a newcomer:** [technical_report.md](technical_report.md) →
[system_architecture.md](system_architecture.md) → [stageA_kp_sweep.md](stageA_kp_sweep.md).
Labels such as W02 (a week of the plan), stage C, PG-2 (a pass/fail gate) or FR-Q3 (a
requirement) are explained in [project_history.md](project_history.md).

## Notes on the evidence

- **The stage A figure** comes from three scripts: `scripts/sweep_gain_robustness.py`
  (simulation), `deploy/tools/probe_cpp/gain_sweep_real.py` (robot) and
  `scripts/plot_gain_sweep.py` (the figure). The raw robot recordings (~80 MB) are not in
  git; `stageA_kp_sweep_real.json` holds their reduced per-recording metrics.
- **The training-milestone figures** are built by `scripts/plot_m1_figures.py` from the
  evaluation reports and tensorboard logs, so they cannot drift from the numbers. The
  reward curves compare the three W04 runs, which share one reward function. W03 is left
  out because its reward terms differ.
- **The walking video** was recorded on a phone, with the audio removed. It shows the FP32
  TensorRT engine at the trained gains (`kp_scale=1.0`) in developer mode. Over 21 s the
  bridge logged obs 50.0 Hz, cmd 500.0 Hz, inference p50 453 µs of the 20,000 µs budget,
  and no failures. It replaces an earlier W06 clip that is kept outside the repository
  because a lab member's face is in frame.
- **`joint_check.md`** is regenerated at the training task's physics step (0.005 s). With
  the hardware gains the robot does not stand under joint PD alone, and that is expected:
  the policy balances it actively.
