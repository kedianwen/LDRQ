# Stage A · `kp_scale` stability domain (the robustness experiment FR-R2 and the sim2real gap FR-R4)

![kp_scale sweep](stageA_kp_sweep.png)

**Controlled perturbation:** every joint's PD stiffness × `kp_scale`, damping unchanged, the
same knob on both sides (the bridge's `kp_scale` launch argument on the robot; per-env actuator
stiffness in simulation). **Same command sequence on both sides:**
`walk 5s@0.3; turn left 180@0.4; walk 5s@0.3; turn left 180@0.4`, generated by `mission_ctl`'s
Executor on both, scored over the commanded window only.

**Stability definition, fixed before measuring:** no DEGRADED (sim: no fall), torso tilt ≤ 20°,
leg tracking rms ≤ 1.5× the kp = 1.0 point, torque below its limit. A real gain is stable only
if every recording at it passes; a sim gain, if ≥ 95 % of its 64 envs pass.

| | stable interval | width | lower edge | upper edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **sim** (64 envs/gain, nominal, no gantry) | 0.80 – 2.00+ | ≥ 1.20 | bounded (0.7 fails tracking; ≤ 0.6 falls while standing) | not reached — grid ends at 2.0, peak torque 86 % |
| **real** (13 recordings, slack gantry) | **1.10 – 1.50** | **0.40** | bounded (1.00 passed once, failed once on a 0.08 s tilt spike) | bounded (1.60 emergency-stopped on audible joint noise) |

**Gap:** lower edge +0.30, upper edge ≤ −0.50. The robot tolerates at most a third of the
gain error the simulator says it should, and the trained gain (1.0), comfortably inside the
simulated interval, sits on the real one's lower edge.

## Where the gap is, metric by metric

- **Leg tracking agrees in shape.** Normalised to each side's own kp = 1.0 point, both fall
  monotonically with stiffness; they differ by at most 0.11 from 1.0 to 1.5 and by up to 0.24
  below (0.7). The joint-level PD response scales with stiffness roughly as the simulator
  predicts. (In absolute terms the real tracking error is ~20 % larger: 0.091 vs 0.076 rad at 1.0.)
- **Posture does not.** The real torso leans far more at every gain — standing tilt 5 – 17°
  against the sim's 1 – 4°, peak tilt 14 – 32° against 3 – 8° — and the lean grows steeply
  below kp = 1.0, where the sim's barely moves. Every real failure on the low side is a tilt
  failure, a criterion the simulator never approaches. The gap on the low side is postural
  (gravity sag of a softer leg), not joint tracking.
- **Peak torque follows the same trend** (real 83 % at 1.5 vs sim 78 %), but the real upper edge
  is audible joint noise at 1.6, something the simulated actuator does not model at all: the
  sim runs cleanly to 2.0.

## Caveats that bound what this says

- The real interval rests on 11 gains and 13 recordings; it states that the *trend* disagrees in
  a specific way, not a precise gap value.
- Absolute real tilt is measured against a calibrated IMU "upright" vector; an error in that
  reference would shift every real tilt by a constant. It cannot produce the slope with kp.
- At kp ≤ 0.6 the simulated robot falls 0.9 – 2.1 s after starting to stand from the default pose, while
  the real robot stood and walked with ~17° lean. The real runs were on a slack gantry, which
  may have carried part of the load at that lean. Unresolved.
- Turn timing is not compared: on the gantry, rope torsion slowed or reversed turns (see
  `deploy/tools/probe_cpp/gain_sweep_real.py`). The stability metrics inside the commanded
  window are unaffected.
- The simulated policy is `exported/policy.pt` of run `2026-08-19_11-03-32_week04_nohead`,
  checked at start-up against `deploy/artifacts/parity_fixture.bin` (max error 1.1e-5), the
  fixture the robot's TensorRT engine matched — so the simulation runs the deployed network.

## Reproduce

```bash
# sim half (~3 min on the 2080 Ti; conda env env_isaaclab) -> outputs/gain_sweep/<date>/results.json
~/IsaacLab/isaaclab.sh -p scripts/sweep_gain_robustness.py --headless
# real half + gap line (recordings are not in git: outputs/gain_sweep_real/2026-09-28/all)
python3 deploy/tools/probe_cpp/gain_sweep_real.py --collect <recordings> --sim <results.json>
# figure, from the committed data (any python with matplotlib)
python3 scripts/plot_gain_sweep.py --sim docs/stageA_kp_sweep_sim.json \
    --real docs/stageA_kp_sweep_real.json --out docs/stageA_kp_sweep.png
```

`stageA_kp_sweep_sim.json` is the simulated `results.json` this figure was drawn from (re-run
from a clean clone on 2026-09-29: identical); `stageA_kp_sweep_real.json` is the per-recording
reduction of the real recordings (`plot_gain_sweep.py --dump-real`).
